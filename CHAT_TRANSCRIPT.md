## Response 12

*Stored order: 340 · 2026-11-10 09:53:38 UTC*

`MUG-01, 1` is one line in the order:

- **MUG** identifies the product type: a mug.
- **01** is that product’s SKU number, distinguishing it from other products.
- **`, 1`** means the quantity is 1.

So `MUG-01, 2` means **two mugs**. The SKU must match a product in the catalog. The demo catalog also has `BOOK-01` (a book) and `LAMP-01` (a lamp).
